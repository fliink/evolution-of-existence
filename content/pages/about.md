Hi, I'm Adam. This is my corner of the internet.

This site is about my life as it relates to the mind, the body and the spirit. What reality is. What our experiences actually are. What matters in this life, as far as we're able to perceive it. Big questions, asked by one ordinary person, in public, over a long time.

It runs along three threads, loosely held:

- **Mind.** The intellectual journey. I have a need to know how everything works, and this is where I chase it.
- **Body.** The fitness journey. Training, recovery, food, sleep, and what the body teaches the rest of me.
- **Spirit.** The spiritual journey. What is real, what experience is, and what deserves our attention.

The threads are a way in, not a filing system. Most of what's worth saying sits where they overlap. There's also a [journey](/journey/) page for the longer arc and a [now](/now/) page for the current snapshot.

## How it's built

*Evolution of Existence* is hand-built and hand-maintained. No platform, no CMS, no algorithm deciding what you see. Just a folder of Markdown files, a few hundred lines of code, and whatever I feel like putting here.
