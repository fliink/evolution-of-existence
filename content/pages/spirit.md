What is reality? What are our experiences, really? What matters in this life, as far as we're able to perceive it? This thread is where I sit with those questions without pretending to have settled them.

One idea that keeps coming back: the moment we label something, we cheapen its existence in our minds. The word stands in for the thing, and we stop looking. That's a strange thought to put at the top of a page called "Spirit", and the tension is part of the point.
