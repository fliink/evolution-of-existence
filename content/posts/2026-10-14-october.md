Lots of things go through my mind this time of year. I reflect on my choices, my habits and my priorities. Around the end of September I hit a mental slump that's hard to pull myself out of. Summer is ending and I start reviewing the list of things I wanted to get done during the summer. Then I start reviewing the list of things I did do.
