The moment we put a label on something, it gets smaller in our minds. "Tree." "Anxiety." "Tuesday." The word arrives and we stop looking at the thing itself, because the word tells us we already know what it is.

- *When did you first notice this happening? A specific moment lands better than a general claim.*
- *Is there a way to use words without letting them do this? Or is the cheapening the price of being able to think at all?*
- *What would it mean to meet something without naming it, even briefly?*
