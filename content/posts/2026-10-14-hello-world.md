Every so often I get the urge to have a place online that is actually *mine*. Not a profile on someone else's platform. Not a feed that decides what matters. Just a domain, some files, and whatever I choose to put in them.

This is that place.

## What this is

**Evolution of Existence** is about my life as it relates to the mind, the body and the spirit. What reality is, what our experiences actually are, and what matters in this life as we perceive it. It runs along three threads: an intellectual journey, because I need to know how everything works; a fitness journey; and a spiritual one. Some of it will be short. Some of it will ramble. All of it will be honest.

## How it's built

There's no CMS behind this. No WordPress, no Substack, no site builder. The whole thing is a small script I wrote that turns a folder of Markdown files into plain HTML, plus one stylesheet. Writing a post means opening a text editor. Publishing means pushing to a repo.

It's deliberately boring technology, and I like it that way. It will still work in ten years.
